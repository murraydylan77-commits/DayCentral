# DayCentral Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 1, 2026
**Developer:** Dylan Murray
**Contact:** murray.dylan77@hotmail.com

DayCentral is a daily planning app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It was built to keep your information on your device. This policy explains what the app accesses, where that information is stored, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- **We don't collect your data.** DayCentral has no accounts on our servers, no cloud sync, and no developer-run backend. We never receive your calendar, reminders, photos, notes, location, or name.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking.** The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and it does not track you across apps or websites.
- **Everything stays on your device.** Information you add or connect is stored locally. Only the system services you choose to use (such as Apple Weather) or a news feed you add receive network requests.
- **You're in control.** You can disconnect any source at any time, and **Settings → Delete all data** removes everything the app has saved.

## Information the app uses

### Sign in with Apple (optional)

You can use DayCentral as a Guest or sign in with Apple. If you sign in, the app asks Apple for **no name and no email address**. It stores only:

- the anonymous user identifier Apple provides, and
- the display name you choose inside the app.

Both are saved in this device's Keychain. They don't sync to iCloud Keychain and are never sent to us. From time to time the app asks Apple whether your sign-in is still valid. If you revoke it, the app switches back to Guest. Signing out clears this information from the device.

### Apple Calendar and Reminders (optional)

If you connect Calendar or Reminders and grant permission, DayCentral reads your events and reminders to build your briefing. This information is **held in memory only** and isn't copied into the app's storage.

If you choose to edit, complete, or delete an event or reminder, the app saves that change to Apple Calendar or Reminders, after asking you to confirm any deletion. Those changes may then sync to your other devices through your Apple account. Apple requires "Full Access" permission for apps to read this data, but DayCentral only reads data to display it and makes changes only when you ask.

A few small preferences are stored locally: which calendars and reminder lists you've picked for your work views, reminders you've chosen to hide, and event categories.

### Location and weather (optional)

If you turn on Weather, the app uses your **approximate location** (within about a kilometer) to request a forecast from Apple Weather. Your location is **not saved** by the app and is never sent to us. Apple processes weather requests under its own privacy policy. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather ([data sources](https://developer.apple.com/weatherkit/data-source-attribution/)).

### Photos (optional)

When you choose a photo for the "Your moments" card, the system photo picker shares **only that single photo** with the app. DayCentral never gets access to your photo library. The app saves a resized copy in its own storage and leaves out the original photo's metadata, such as location and camera details. Removing the photo in the app does not delete the original from your library.

### News feeds (optional)

If you add a news feed, the app connects directly to the feed address you enter, and that publisher receives a normal web request, including your IP address. The app doesn't store cookies for these requests. Opening an article takes you to the publisher's website, where their privacy policy applies. The feed address you choose is saved on your device.

### Things you add in the app

Custom cards, notes, checklists, work items, meal plans, movement check-ins, card layouts, and app settings are saved **only on your device**. They're not uploaded to us or to any server.

Like other app data, this information may be included in your device backups (for example, iCloud Backup) according to your device settings.

### App lock (optional)

- **Face ID and Touch ID** are handled entirely by Apple. The app never receives your biometric data. If biometrics aren't available, your device passcode or Mac password may be used instead.
- **An app passcode** is never stored as plain text. It's stored as a salted, securely derived hash in this device's Keychain, along with a limit on failed attempts.

App lock controls access to the app's screens. It is not a separate encryption layer for app data.

### Purchases

DayCentral Premium is purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple handles all payments, and we never see your payment details. The app checks your verified App Store transactions only to decide whether Premium features are unlocked. Purchases are tied to your App Store account, not to the optional Sign in with Apple identity.

## Information we collect

**None.** We don't operate servers that receive your data, and we don't use third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking services.

If you choose to share crash reports or diagnostics with developers in your device's settings, Apple may provide us with anonymized technical reports. These are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

If you contact us for support, we use what you send (such as your email address and message) only to reply. Please don't include private calendar, reminder, or photo details unless they're needed to solve your issue.

## Sharing your information

We don't sell, rent, or share your personal information, because we never receive it. The only third parties involved are services you choose to use:

| Service | When it's used | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| Apple (Sign in with Apple, Weather, App Store) | Only when you use those features | What Apple needs to provide the service, under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) |
| News publishers | Only for feeds you add | A standard web request to the feed or article you open |

## Your choices and controls

- **Disconnect sources** at any time in **Connections**. This clears that source's data from the app.
- **Remove your saved photo** from the photo card or Connections.
- **Clear meal plans** in **Settings → Your data**.
- **Delete all data** in **Settings → Your data**. After you confirm, this removes all content, preferences, connections, your saved sign-in and display name, and app lock settings that DayCentral stores on this device. It does not delete original items in Apple Calendar, Reminders, or Photos, and it doesn't cancel subscriptions.
- **Revoke permissions** for Calendar, Reminders, Location, or Face ID at any time in your device's **Settings → Privacy & Security**.
- **Manage or cancel subscriptions** in your Apple account settings.

Deleting the app also removes its locally stored data. Copies may remain in existing device backups.

## Children's privacy

DayCentral is not directed at children under 13, and because we don't collect personal information, we don't knowingly collect information from children.
